Repaper — Paper-Fast Local Note Taking App & Markdown Notes Workspace

Repaper is an ultra-fast, local-first paper-like note taking workspace combining the distraction-free tactile speed of paper notebooks with the privacy, data ownership, and graph connectivity of modern markdown knowledge bases. Write notes, organize thoughts, link ideas with bidirectional wikilinks, and visualize your digital brain with interactive graph views.

Paper-Like Fluid Note Taking

Experience instant, distraction-free writing that feels as responsive as writing on physical paper. Clean typography, customizable page covers, and zero lag.

Local-First Markdown Notes

Every note remains on your machine. True offline-first notes with zero cloud lock-in, zero tracking, and 100% data portability via standard .md files.

Connected Notes & Knowledge Graph

Connect ideas organically with [[wikilinks]] and navigate your personal knowledge network with Cytoscape-powered interactive note graphs.

STEM Notes: LaTeX & Diagrams

Write technical notes with native LaTeX math equations via KaTeX and build live flowcharts, architecture diagrams, and mind maps via Mermaid.js.

Frequently Asked Questions About Repaper Note Taking

What is Repaper?

Repaper is an ultra-fast, local-first paper-like note taking application and markdown notes workspace designed for developers, students, researchers, and creators.

Why choose Repaper over physical paper notebooks?

Repaper delivers the immediate speed and tactile joy of physical paper note taking without the downsides: your notes are instantly searchable, connected via wikilinks, rendered with live diagrams and math formulas, and stored securely as plain markdown files.

Is Repaper free and offline for note taking?

Yes. Repaper is free, open source under the MIT license, and fully offline-capable. All notes are saved to your local device.

How does Repaper compare to Notion and Obsidian for note taking?

Repaper combines Notion-style block editing and aesthetic covers with Obsidian-style local file privacy and bidirectional knowledge graphs—without monthly fees, proprietary databases, or complex setup.
