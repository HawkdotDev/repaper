Repaper — Free Local-First Hybrid Block & Markdown Knowledge Base

Repaper is an offline-first personal workspace combining the aesthetic fluidity of Notion with the privacy and data ownership of Obsidian. Write, organize, connect thoughts with bidirectional wikilinks, and visualize ideas with interactive graph views.

Local-First & Offline

Your notes stay on your machine. Zero cloud dependency, zero subscriptions, and 100% data portability using open standard Markdown files.

Hybrid Block-Markdown Editor

Enjoy rich Notion-like blocks, checklists, page banners, and icons without sacrificing raw markdown compatibility.

Interactive Knowledge Graph

Connect ideas with [[wikilinks]] and navigate your personal knowledge network with Cytoscape-powered interactive graphs.

LaTeX Math & Mermaid Diagrams

Write complex equations with KaTeX and build flowcharts, architecture diagrams, and sequence charts with Mermaid.js.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Repaper free and open source?

Yes. Repaper is free, open source under the MIT license, and fully offline-capable.

Where are my notes saved?

In web mode, notes are safely persisted in your browser IndexedDB filesystem. In desktop mode, notes reside in your local folder as standard .md files.
